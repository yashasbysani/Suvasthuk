## What is the construction cost per sq ft in Bangalore in 2026?

As of early 2026, typical market bands for residential construction in Bengaluru fall into three levels:

- **Budget / standard construction: roughly ₹1,900 – ₹2,300 per sq ft.** Solid RCC frame, standard vitrified tiles, basic sanitaryware and electricals, cement-finish or basic paint exteriors.
- **Mid-range construction: roughly ₹2,300 – ₹2,900 per sq ft.** Better elevation treatment, branded fittings (Jaquar/Kohler tier), UPVC windows, false ceilings in living areas, higher-grade flooring.
- **Premium construction: ₹2,900 – ₹3,800+ per sq ft.** Architect-detailed elevations, imported or large-format flooring, home automation provisions, premium kitchens and bathrooms, landscape and compound work of matching quality.

Treat these as planning bands, not quotes. Steel, cement, and labour move quarter to quarter, and site conditions (soil, access, water table) shift the number for the same design. For a written estimate against your actual drawings, [talk to our construction team](/construction) — estimates against drawings are the only numbers worth signing.

A serious quote should itemize, at minimum: excavation and foundation, RCC structure (footings, columns, beams, slabs), blockwork/brickwork, internal and external plastering, flooring, doors and windows, electrical wiring and points, plumbing lines and fixtures, painting, and waterproofing of wet areas and terrace.

Where budgets leak is what the rate does **not** include, and Bangalore quotes vary wildly here:

- Compound wall and main gate
- Borewell, sump, and overhead tank
- BBMP plan sanction and government fees ([see our approval guide](/blog/bbmp-plan-approval-bangalore-guide-2025))
- BESCOM and BWSSB connection charges
- Rainwater harvesting system (mandatory for most Bengaluru sites)
- Modular kitchen, wardrobes, and all loose furniture
- Lift, solar water heating, home automation

Ask every builder for a written inclusion/exclusion list before comparing rates. A ₹2,100 quote with twenty exclusions routinely ends up costlier than a ₹2,400 quote with five.

Rates are quoted per sq ft of **built-up area**, so the total depends on how much you build:

- A **30×40 site (1,200 sq ft)** with ground + first floor is typically 2,000–2,400 sq ft built-up → roughly **₹46–62 lakh** at mid-range rates. We cover this site size in detail in our [30×40 construction guide](/blog/30x40-house-construction-bangalore).
- A **40×60 site (2,400 sq ft)** duplex of 3,500–4,200 sq ft built-up → roughly **₹85 lakh – 1.2 crore** mid-range.
- Each additional floor spreads foundation and roof costs across more area, so cost per sq ft usually **drops slightly** with G+2 versus G+1 — one reason rental-income floors often make financial sense in Bengaluru.

## Why do two quotes for the same house differ by 30%?

Because they are rarely for the same house. The usual differences: steel quantity per sq ft (structure designed to IS codes versus "as per thumb rule"), concrete grade, wall thickness, brand tiers for every fitting, and whether supervision is a dedicated site engineer or a weekly visit. When you compare quotes, you are comparing specifications — insist on seeing them side by side.

This is also the strongest argument for the design-build model: when the people who drew your house also price and build it, the estimate is built from the actual drawings, and there is no gap for "extras" to hide in. We explain the difference in [design-build vs traditional contracting](/blog/design-build-vs-traditional-contractor-bangalore).

## How to keep your build on budget — five rules from 30 years on site

1. **Finish the design before you start the site.** Every change after slab-casting costs three times what it would have cost on paper.
2. **Fix the specification list, not just the rate.** Brand and model for every visible fitting, grade for everything structural.
3. **Stage your payments against measured work,** never against calendar dates.
4. **Keep a 10% contingency** — genuinely unforeseen items (rock excavation, water table) exist even on well-planned sites.
5. **Put supervision in the contract.** A dedicated site engineer is the cheapest insurance you can buy.

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does it cost to build a 1,200 sq ft house in Bangalore in 2026?**
For 1,200 sq ft of built-up area, expect roughly ₹23–28 lakh at standard specification, ₹28–35 lakh mid-range, and ₹35 lakh+ premium — construction only, excluding land, approvals, compound work, borewell, and interiors. Most owners on a 30×40 site build two floors rather than one, which roughly doubles built-up area while improving the per-sq-ft economics, so budget against the total area you will actually build, not the plot size.

**Is it cheaper to hire a contractor directly instead of an architecture firm?**
It looks cheaper on day one and frequently costs more by handover. Without detailed drawings and a fixed specification, the contractor prices low, then bills "extras" for everything the quote never defined. A design-build firm gives you drawings, a specification, and a price that all describe the same house — the savings come from what does not go wrong.

**What is the cost of construction materials in Bangalore right now?**
As of early 2026: cement roughly ₹380–430 per bag, TMT steel roughly ₹62,000–70,000 per tonne, M-sand and aggregates varying by zone and transport distance. Material prices move monthly, which is why any quote older than 60 days should be revalidated before you sign.

**Does the per-sq-ft rate include interiors?**
No — in Bengaluru practice, "construction cost" ends at painted walls, flooring, and fitted bathrooms. Modular kitchen, wardrobes, false ceilings beyond basics, and loose furniture are an interiors budget, typically another ₹400–900 per sq ft depending on scope.
