## 5. On-page checklist (apply to every page/post — mirrors the audit)

- Title ≤60 chars before the brand suffix; one H1; question-form H2s where natural.
- Meta description 140–155 chars, includes Bengaluru/Karnataka + a reason to click.
- FAQ section (3–5 Q&As) with substantive 60–120 word answers → wire into `FAQPage` JSON-LD (component pattern already exists on service pages; extend to blog template — audit GEO finding).
- Byline: **B K Muralidhar, Principal Architect** (fix `Article.author` Person/Organization mismatch).
- 3+ contextual internal links in body (service page, related post, /projects or /construction) + one CTA block.
- 1–2 authoritative outbound links (BBMP/OBPAS, RERA Karnataka, BESCOM/BWSSB, NBC) where claims are made.
- Images: real project photos via staging paths where possible; AI-generated only for illustrative diagrams, labeled as such — **never presented as our built work**. Alt text per CATEGORY-MAPPING.md §3.
- Body uses only headings/paragraphs/lists/bold (clean Portable Text migration; no tables in post bodies).
- ₹ figures always hedged: "as of early 2026, typical Bengaluru market ranges" + CTA for an exact estimate.
